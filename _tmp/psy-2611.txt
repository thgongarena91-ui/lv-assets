[size=130][color=#8FE3FF][b]WHAT A PSYCHOVERSE IS[/b][/color][/size]

[color=white]A [/color][color=white][b]Psychoverse[/b][/color][color=white] is a world you enter by taking a drug. Each [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=1999868][color=#7FDBFF]Zyx[/color][/url][color=white] drug carries one world: a casino where nobody loses, a film you live as the hero, a party that never ends, the [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832719][color=#7FDBFF]Hermosa[/color][/url][color=white] of 1999. You take the dose, close your eyes and wake up inside. You see it, hear it, touch it and taste it, and you meet everyone else who took the same drug at the same time. To you it feels as real as the street outside. Many say it feels more real.[/color]

[color=white][b]How it works.[/b][/color][color=white] Three things make a Psychoverse. The [/color][color=white][b]dose[/b][/color][color=white], made from the [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832745][color=#7FDBFF]Xunaya[/color][/url][color=white] flower, puts the body to sleep and keeps the mind awake and open. The [/color][color=white][b]world[/b][/color][color=white] is designed by Zyx engineers, like a film set nobody ever has to build. The [/color][color=white][b]sync[/b][/color][color=white] keeps every dreamer in the same world: chemistry alone can hold a shared dream for a few hundred people, as it did in the Xinko villages, but not for millions. For that, Zyx runs artificial minds on the [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2830614][color=#7FDBFF]ZyxNet[/color][/url][color=white] that hold the world steady and keep each mind from pulling it its own way. Zyx has never said where those minds came from.[/color]

[color=white][b]The body stays behind.[/b][/color][color=white] While the mind is inside, the body lies still wherever the dose was taken: a bed, a [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=1999868][color=#7FDBFF]RestBox[/color][/url][color=white] capsule, a booth in a Dreamhall Parlor rented by the hour. It still gets hungry, thirsty and old. That is why the Connected come back to the real world at all: to work, eat, sleep and buy the next dose. Doses are sold one by one or by [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832746][color=#7FDBFF]Zyx Experience[/color][/url][color=white] subscription; since 2073 the basic Psychoverse of each city has been free.[/color]

[color=white][b]Waking up is the hard part.[/b][/color][color=white] The longer you stay, the harder it is to tell the dream from the street; too many hours lead to [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832732][color=#7FDBFF]Rupture[/color][/url][color=white]. Two things protect the mind: [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832746][color=#7FDBFF]Ancorine[/color][/url][color=white], which keeps [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832688][color=#7FDBFF]Anchor Squad[/color][/url][color=white] agents anchored to the real world, and the [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832720][color=#7FDBFF]Kizuna Core[/color][/url][color=white], an implant that makes the brain immune to Zyx drugs.[/color]

[size=130][color=#8FE3FF][b]FROM TEA TO DRUG[/b][/color][/size]

[color=white]For centuries the [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832744][color=#7FDBFF]Xinko-Xenko[/color][/url][color=white] elders brewed the Xunaya on sacred nights and a whole village dreamed the same dream. In 2023 Zyx turned that ritual into chemistry: [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832735][color=#7FDBFF][b]Eferverso[/b][/color][/url][color=white] let the survivors of Hermosa share a rebuilt city, [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832735][color=#7FDBFF]Universalia[/color][/url][color=white], that exists only in their minds. It was the first [/color][color=white][b]Psychoactive Reality[/b][/color][color=white] drug. Today Zyx lists about three hundred.[/color]
[/td][/tr][/table]

[size=130][color=#8FE3FF][b]THE REALITY WAR (2030)[/b][/color][/size]

[color=white]Zyx was not the only company selling other worlds. [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832712][color=#7FDBFF]Sphere[/color][/url][color=white], led by [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=1999876][color=#7FDBFF]Iris Stuart[/color][/url][color=white], had built [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2830614][color=#7FDBFF][b]Horizon[/b][/color][/url][color=white], the most advanced virtual reality network ever made, and lobbied the [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=1999866][color=#7FDBFF]Cabinet[/color][/url][color=white] to ban P.R. as a narcotic. The [/color][color=white][b]Reality War[/b][/color][color=white] was fought in the Cabinet, the courts, the press — and finally with sabotage. It ended with the [/color][color=white][b]Horizon Blackout[/b][/color][color=white]: one attack disconnected millions of Sphere users at the same second.[/color]

[color=white]Nobody proved who did it, but investigators found familiar fingerprints: the hackers who had destroyed Hermosa were alive, and working for Zyx. President [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2557482][color=#7FDBFF]Harrison Blackstone[/color][/url][color=white] shelved every ban. Sphere went bankrupt in 2032; [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=1999868][color=#7FDBFF]Automa[/color][/url][color=white] bought its AI cores and [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=1999868][color=#7FDBFF]Kizuna[/color][/url][color=white] its brain-machine technology.[/color]
[/td][/tr][/table]

[table=plain][tr][td]
[size=130][color=#8FE3FF][b]RUPTURE[/b][/color][/size]

[color=white]In 2033 an unemployed actor who spent eighteen hours a day in the [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832746][color=#7FDBFF]Cinemaphica[/color][/url][color=white] Psychoverse killed fourteen people in [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2530927][color=#7FDBFF]Valewood[/color][/url][color=white], convinced he was still inside a film. Doctors called it [/color][color=white][b]Rupture[/b][/color][color=white]: the mind loses the border between dream and world. The streets called its victims [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2832732][color=#7FDBFF][b]dreamwalkers[/b][/color][/url][color=white]. Neighborhood militias began hunting anyone who seemed "out of reality", until in 2035 the Cabinet created the [/color][color=white][b]Anchor Squad[/b][/color][color=white] — funded by Zyx, which also makes the Ancorine every agent depends on.[/color]
[/td][/tr][/table]

[table=plain][tr][td]
[size=130][color=#8FE3FF][b]TOTAL CONNECTION[/b][/color][/size]

[color=white]In 2073 President [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=2557482][color=#7FDBFF]Hiroshi Tanabe[/color][/url][color=white], a former Zyx director, made P.R. free as a "social benefit" and gave every city its own Psychoverse. The country split between the [/color][color=white][b]Connected[/b][/color][color=white] and the [/color][color=white][b]Awake[/b][/color][color=white]. Since then, a week rarely passes without a dreamwalker shot in one of the seven cities. In 2088 Zyx proposed the final step: one Psychoverse for everyone — the [/color][url=https://www.nationstates.net/page=dispatch/id=1999872][color=#7FDBFF]Dream Vision[/color][/url][color=white].[/color]
